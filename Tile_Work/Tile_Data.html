treatment_daily_summary <- daily_max_df %>%
  group_by(Date, Treatment) %>%
  summarise(avg_max_temp = mean(max_temp, na.rm = TRUE), .groups = "drop") %>%
  # Pivot treatments into columns to compute the difference
  pivot_wider(names_from = Treatment, values_from = avg_max_temp) %>%
  mutate(
    # Absolute difference between black and white treatment averages
    temp_diff = abs(Black - White),
    # Signed difference (positive means Black was hotter, negative means White was hotter)
    black_minus_white = Black - White
  )
avg_offset <- mean(treatment_daily_summary$black_minus_white, na.rm = TRUE)

Tide Notes for Winter sites- not concerned about tides not heating tiles enough

By end of December, tides (Diamond Head) are in mid afternoon (1-3) Before that, there’s a higher low in the am about 8 hrs before the low low, usually around the time the summer tides have been in the am, but only at about 0.2-0 as lowest values. Same ish with Sandy’s and Ewa with afternoon lows right at 0 by mid-end of Dec.
