Field Work

Daily Notes

2/22/26 :

10:45 on the sand

0.0 tide at 11:34 am

Excursion to Turtle Bay! We got to the Ritz ~10:30, and were down on the sand ~10:45. The tide wasn’t as low as I expected. Celia, Natalie and I began by identifying the GPS coordinate we had, and then took some site photos. Tide was difficult to work with. We ended up setting out 8 vertical transects between 4 and 9 ish meters long to capture zonation across the site. GPS Points (Named T01 and T08 MB) were taken at the first and last transect points and rough GPS coordinates are captured with each photo as well. After those, we set up a 50 m transect roughly between those two points running parallel to the shore and took photoquads every 2 m. All photoquads can be found here. I was worried I didn’t capture the best area, so I did some looking closer towards the point. I found similar algae comp and a few rock boring urchins, but no study species, and the rock was less consistent. I did find one Opihi at approx m 43 on our parallel transect! We attempted to scout out Laniakea a little, but parking was practically impossible. I uploaded all photos to my shared Madigan Boborci Drive in a Field Work Photos Folder on 2/23/26. They are also being uploaded to the lab desktop and are on Koa Cloud since we don’t currently have hard drives.
